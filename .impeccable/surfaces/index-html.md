Scope: aharoll.com root (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Shopify merchants / merch leads, 20 to 2,000 SKUs, on a laptop mid-admin. Action: install Asset Audit from the Shopify App Store. Proof: a free, browser-only scan of their own public catalog. Constraints: no invented reviews, logos or metrics; App Store pricing ($20/mo for 100 products, $200/yr); never imply the free scan is the AI audit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog as a swatch fan guide. Each blade is one product: its gallery as chips, missing shots as hatched empty chips, a severity band at the tip. Refuses the category default of a split hero with a dashboard screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Viewing-booth neutral gray ground, white blades with hairline rules, black cover blade, severity as the only saturated color (vermilion, orange, ochre, sage). One grotesk (Archivo) across widths: expanded caps for codes and headings, normal width for reading. Rivets, blade codes (COV 35 / VAR 40 / SEO 25), hatched empties.

STORY: See your catalog fanned out by what's broken, understand that missing and mismatched shots cost sales, scan your own store free, install for the AI vision audit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline top-left over the fan; fan pivot bottom-center, 9 to 15 blades spreading about 120 degrees; black cover blade upright at center holds the store URL field and Scan button; Install on Shopify in the nav.

FORM: Swatch fan (catalog challenger assigned by roll, user-locked), seed cb7349ef.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
