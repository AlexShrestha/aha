AhaRoll App Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-13

AhaRoll processes merchant-provided product data and campaign briefs to generate creative assets (images/videos). We collect only data required to operate the app, provide support, and improve reliability.

Data we process

  • Shop domain and app installation metadata
  • Selected product references and creative briefs
  • Generated asset metadata and review notes
  • Usage and credit events

How we use data

  • To generate and deliver requested assets
  • To support revisions and approval workflows
  • To provide billing/credit accounting
  • To maintain security, logs, and abuse prevention

Data sharing

We do not sell merchant data. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers needed to run the service.

Retention

Data is retained as needed for service delivery, legal obligations, and support operations.
